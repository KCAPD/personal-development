KCA Personal Development — 4.0 Step 4
PROVISIONAL CURRICULUM MAPPING FOR STAFF REVIEW

PURPOSE
This build gives staff a populated starting point ahead of the Personal Development mapping meeting.

WHAT HAS BEEN ADDED
- Years 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 now have expandable observable behaviours.
- Each behaviour has provisional:
  * Signature experiences
  * Across our curriculum
  * Everyday school life
  * Why this matters
- Year 3 retains the detailed mapping already developed.

SOURCE STANDARD
- Named experiences, project questions, subjects, presentations and audiences have been drawn from the existing KCA yearly curriculum overviews and Journey Journal.
- Where a curriculum map contains an uncertain placeholder (for example "???" trips), it has NOT been added.
- Everyday School Life and Why This Matters are draft interpretive wording, intended for staff to agree, edit or replace.
- The current mapping is not intended to claim that every documented experience develops every behaviour equally. It is deliberately a starting point for staff review.

CURRENT ARCHITECTURE
- EYFS: foundations
- Years 1–6: complete observable behaviours
- Years 1–6: expandable evidence/mapping content
- Year 3: detailed previously agreed mapping
