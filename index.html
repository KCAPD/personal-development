EYFS · Nursery & Reception

Building the Foundations

In Nursery and Reception, children are immersed in the KCA values through play, relationships, routines, communication and exploration. The focus is on developing a secure, age-appropriate understanding of what each value looks and feels like in everyday life, creating the foundations for the observable behaviours that follow from Year 1 onwards.

Integrity

Beginning to make positive choices, follow routines and understand why expectations matter.

Respect

Learning to listen, share, notice difference and understand that everyone belongs.

Endurance

Learning to keep trying, manage feelings with support and grow in independence.

Kindness

Helping, sharing, including others and showing care for people, animals and the environment.

Courage

Trying new things, joining in, sharing ideas and becoming increasingly confident.

Aspiration

Exploring interests, asking questions and becoming curious about the wider world.

Courage · Finding and using my voice

What Courage feels like at KCA

At King's Cross Academy, courage is about having the confidence to be yourself, share your ideas and embrace challenge. Children are encouraged to take risks in their learning, perform in front of others and understand that mistakes are a natural part of becoming a successful learner.
